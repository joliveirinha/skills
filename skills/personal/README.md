# Personal

Skills for personal life — planning, organizing, and everyday decisions outside of work.

## The skills

| Skill | Invoke with | Use it… |
|---|---|---|
| **plan-trip** | `/plan-trip` | To plan a vacation or activity trip. An expert travel planner that interviews you about who's going (ages, interests), theme, season, origin, duration, and budget, then proposes destinations, flights, lodging, and age-appropriate activities — iterating with you until the plan fits. |

## Notes

- **plan-trip is conversational** — nothing is saved to disk; the plan lives in the chat. Keep
  the session open while you iterate.
- It **uses web research when available** (real destinations, hotels, activities, ballpark
  prices) and always reminds you to verify live price and availability before booking. Without
  web tools it plans from general knowledge and flags what to check.

_More personal skills will be added over time._
